## 1. Overview

If you have questions not covered here, contact your HR Business Partner or reach out to the People Operations team at `hr@contoso.com`.

## 3. Employment Terms

### 3.1 Probation Period

- All new employees serve a **6-month probation period**
- Performance is reviewed at the 3-month and 6-month marks
- Managers must submit a probation review form via Workday
- Successful completion is confirmed via a formal letter from HR
- During probation, the notice period is **2 weeks** (both parties)

### 3.2 Notice Period (Post-Probation)

| Band | Notice Period |
|------|-------------|
| Junior (Bands 1–3) | 4 weeks |
| Mid-level (Bands 4–6) | 8 weeks |
| Senior (Bands 7–9) | 12 weeks |
| Director and above | 3–6 months (per contract) |

### 3.3 Working Hours

- **Standard hours:** 37.5 hours per week (Monday–Friday)
- **Core hours:** 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (all employees must be available during core hours)
- Flexible start/end times outside core hours are available — agree with your manager
- Overtime for non-exempt employees is compensated at **1.5x standard rate** with manager approval
- Time tracking is done in Workday for all hourly and project-billed roles

## 4. Leave Entitlements

### 4.1 Annual Leave

| Employee Band | Annual Leave | Public Holidays |
|--------------|-------------|-----------------|
| All employees (Year 1) | 25 days | 8 days |
| After 3 years | 27 days | 8 days |
| After 5 years | 30 days | 8 days |
| Senior Manager and above | 30 days from start | 8 days |

**Annual Leave Rules:**
- Leave year runs **1 January – 31 December**
- Up to **5 days** can be carried over to the following year — must be used by 31 March
- Leave must be booked at least **2 weeks in advance** for periods of 5+ consecutive days
- Leave requests are submitted and approved in Workday
- Leave cannot be taken in the first 4 weeks of employment without manager approval

### 4.2 Sick Leave

- **Paid sick leave:** Up to **10 days per rolling 12-month period** with no doctor's note required
- For absences exceeding 3 consecutive days, a **fit note from a GP** is required
- For absences exceeding 10 days in a 12-month period, a return-to-work meeting with HR is required
- Long-term illness (28+ days): Occupational health referral and statutory sick pay procedures apply
- All sick leave must be reported via Workday or by calling your manager on Day 1 of absence

### 4.3 Parental Leave

**Maternity Leave:**
- Up to **52 weeks** in total (26 weeks Ordinary Maternity Leave + 26 weeks Additional Maternity Leave)
- **Enhanced pay:** 16 weeks at full pay, 10 weeks at 50% pay, remaining weeks at statutory rate
- Must give at least 15 weeks' notice of intended leave start date

**Paternity Leave:**
- **2 weeks** paid at full rate
- Must be taken within 56 days of the birth or adoption date
- Submit via Workday under "Parental Leave Request"

**Shared Parental Leave:**
- Available after maternity leave has been curtailed
- Up to **50 weeks** of leave can be shared between parents
- Enhanced pay applies for the first 12 weeks of shared leave
- Contact HR at least 8 weeks before intended start date

**Adoption Leave:**
- Same entitlements as maternity leave apply to the primary adopter
- Secondary adopter receives paternity leave equivalent

### 4.4 Other Leave Types

| Leave Type | Entitlement | Policy |
|-----------|------------|--------|
| **Compassionate Leave** | Up to 5 days paid | Immediate family bereavement. Additional unpaid leave available at manager discretion |
| **Dependants Emergency Leave** | Up to 3 days per year | Unpaid. For emergency care of dependants only |
| **Study/Exam Leave** | Up to 5 days paid | With prior HR approval and evidence of enrolment |
| **Jury Service** | Full pay for duration | Submit jury summons to HR immediately |
| **Volunteering Leave** | 2 days per year paid | For approved charity/community activities |
| **Sabbatical** | Up to 3 months unpaid | After 5 years of service, with 3 months' notice and manager approval |

## 5. Benefits

### 5.1 Health & Wellbeing

**Private Medical Insurance (PMI):**
- All employees are enrolled in Contoso's PMI scheme from **Day 1**
- Provider: **Bupa**
- Cover includes: GP access, specialist consultations, diagnostics, inpatient and outpatient treatment
- Dependants (partner and children under 18) can be added at a subsidised rate — enrol within 30 days of start date or a life event
- Dental and optical cover are included in the standard plan

**Mental Health Support:**
- **Employee Assistance Programme (EAP):** Free, confidential counselling — up to 8 sessions per year
- EAP Helpline: `0800-CONTOSO-EAP` (available 24/7)
- Mental Health First Aiders are available in every office — contact HR for the list

**Gym & Fitness:**
- Subsidised gym membership available through **Gympass** — 50% of monthly cost covered by Contoso
- On-site gym available at the London HQ office (open 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM)

### 5.2 Pension

- **Pension Provider:** Aviva
- **Employee contribution:** Minimum 5% of salary
- **Employer contribution:** 7% of salary (Contoso matches up to 7%)
- Auto-enrolment applies from Day 1 — you will be enrolled automatically at the minimum contribution rate
- To change your contribution rate: log into Workday → Payroll → Pension Settings
- Pension scheme information is available on the Aviva portal — login details in your welcome pack

### 5.3 Life Assurance

- All employees receive **4x annual salary** in life assurance cover from Day 1
- Cover is automatic — no medical questionnaire required
- Provider: Legal & General
- Beneficiary nomination can be completed in Workday → Benefits → Life Assurance

### 5.4 Other Benefits

| Benefit | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Cycle to Work Scheme** | Save up to 42% on a new bike via salary sacrifice |
| **Season Ticket Loan** | Interest-free loan for annual public transport passes |
| **Remote Work Allowance** | £300 per year for home office equipment (receipts required) |
| **Learning Budget** | £1,500 per year for professional development (courses, certifications, conferences) |
| **Childcare Vouchers** | Via salary sacrifice scheme — Tax-Free Childcare eligible |
| **Employee Discounts** | Perkbox platform — access via the HR intranet |
| **Referral Bonus** | £1,500 for a successful employee referral (paid after 6 months' service of referred employee) |

## 8. Code of Conduct & Workplace Policies

### 8.1 Equality, Diversity & Inclusion

Contoso is committed to providing a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, and bullying. All employees are expected to treat colleagues with respect regardless of age, gender, race, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic.

To report a concern: contact HR confidentially at `confidential.hr@contoso.com` or speak with your HR Business Partner.

### 8.2 Remote & Hybrid Working Policy

- Contoso operates a **hybrid working model** — a minimum of **2 days per week in office** is expected
- Some roles may require more frequent office attendance — check your contract
- Remote working from abroad is permitted for up to **30 working days per year** with prior manager approval
- Requests for permanent remote arrangements must be made formally via HR

### 8.3 Expense Policy Summary

|----------|-------|------------------|

| Travel (UK) | Economy class only | Yes |

All expenses must be submitted within **30 days** of being incurred. Submit via Workday → Expenses.

## 10. Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: When will I receive my first payslip?**  
A: Payslips are available in Workday from the 20th of each month. Your first payment will be on the last working day of your first full month of employment.

**Q: How many days of annual leave do I get in my first year?**  
A: All employees receive 25 days of annual leave plus 8 public holidays in their first year. This increases to 27 days after 3 years of service, and 30 days after 5 years.

**Q: Can I carry over unused annual leave?**  
A: Yes — up to 5 days can be carried forward to the following year, but they must be used by 31 March or they will be forfeited.

**Q: How do I enrol in the pension scheme?**  
A: You are automatically enrolled from Day 1 at the minimum 5% contribution rate. To change your contribution level, go to Workday → Payroll → Pension Settings. Contoso contributes 7% on top of your contribution.

**Q: What is the learning budget and how do I use it?**  
A: Every employee receives £1,500 per financial year for professional development. Submit course fees and receipts via Workday → Expenses → Learning & Development. Prior manager approval is required for items over £500.

**Q: How do I report a workplace concern or complaint?**  
A: For sensitive issues, email `confidential.hr@contoso.com`. Your message is only seen by a senior HR representative. Alternatively, speak with any HR Business Partner in confidence.

**Q: Can I work from home full time?**  
A: Contoso's hybrid policy requires a minimum of 2 days per week in the office. Fully remote arrangements require a formal request to HR and are subject to business needs and manager approval.

**Q: How long is my probation period?**  
A: The standard probation period is 6 months, with reviews at the 3-month and 6-month marks. During probation, notice periods are 2 weeks for both parties.

**Q: Where do I find the Employee Handbook?**  
A: The Employee Handbook is available on the Contoso Intranet under the HR Policies section, and also in the New Joiner Hub at `intranet.contoso.com/new-joiners`.

**Q: How do I book time off?**  
A: Log into Workday, click "Time Off", select "Request", choose your dates, and submit for manager approval. You will receive an email confirmation once approved.
